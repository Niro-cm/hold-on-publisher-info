# Hold On Publisher

Hold On Publisher is a private, locally operated automation tool developed to manage the **Hold On** educational YouTube channel.

The application helps its owner prepare and upload original educational YouTube Shorts, manage video titles and descriptions, and retrieve channel and video performance information through the YouTube API Services.

## How It Works

- The application runs locally on the channel owner's computer.
- It uses Google OAuth 2.0 to obtain authorized access to the owner's YouTube channel.
- It uploads approved videos and manages their metadata.
- It retrieves YouTube channel and video information for reporting and analytics.
- It is not a public service and does not offer accounts or access to third-party users.

## Access

Hold On Publisher is intended solely for use by its developer and channel owner. It is not publicly accessible or available for registration.

# Privacy Policy — Hold On Publisher

**Last updated: October 24, 2026**

## Overview

Hold On Publisher is a private, locally operated application used by its developer to manage the Hold On educational YouTube channel. The application is not offered to the public and does not provide third-party user accounts.

## Information Accessed

With authorization through Google OAuth 2.0, Hold On Publisher accesses YouTube account and channel information, including:

- YouTube channel identification and information.
- Video information and metadata.
- Permissions needed to upload videos to the authorized channel.
- YouTube performance information available through the authorized APIs.

## How Information Is Used

The application uses this information solely to upload and manage videos, verify publishing results, prevent duplicate uploads, and generate performance reports for the channel owner.

## Data Storage and Security

OAuth credentials and authorization tokens are stored locally on the developer's computer and are not intentionally published or shared with third parties.

The application may store video identifiers, publishing records, and analytics data locally for operational purposes.

## Data Sharing

Hold On Publisher does not sell personal information or provide YouTube account data to advertisers. Data is exchanged with Google and YouTube only as necessary to operate the authorized API features.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Local operational records are retained as needed for publishing and reporting. The channel owner can remove locally stored data and revoke the application's Google account access.

## Revoking Access

Access can be revoked at any time through [Google Account third-party connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).

## Google API Services

Hold On Publisher's use of information received from Google APIs is subject to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including applicable Limited Use requirements.

## Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the developer through the Hold On YouTube channel: [@holdonstudio](https://www.youtube.com/@holdonstudio).
